Dig Together

The second game on the same live-stream engine. The whole chat digs one shared shaft. Finds are revealed by rarity under the finder's name, and a channel museum and each viewer's collection book carry over from stream to stream.

  • Shared engine pulled out into a local UPM package, reused by both games.
  • Load-tested at 500 comments per minute with 50,000 viewers. Big saves stream to a worker thread, so frame time stays flat.
  • Japanese, English and Arabic at launch. Props and finds come from a scripted Blender pipeline.
  • Unity
  • UPM packages
  • Localization
  • Performance testing
  • Blender Python
